# Code of Conduct

LLMSim is for anyone who tests software that talks to language models, whatever their background or experience. Everyone taking part in this project's issues, discussions, pull requests and reviews is expected to keep it a place where people can do that comfortably. This code is adapted from the ideas in the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Be patient with questions, and assume good faith until shown otherwise.
- Give feedback that is specific and kind, and take it the same way.
- Stay on the project's subject. Keep promotion, politics and personal disputes elsewhere.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or demeaning comments, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or attention, and unwelcome contact.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as an address or an email, without their permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock issues and discussions, and block people from the repository when conduct breaks this code. We will try to say why.

To report a problem, use the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**, and start the title with "Conduct:". Only the maintainers see it, and we will keep your report private. (If the report is about a maintainer, say so, and we will make sure someone else handles it.)

## Scope

This applies in the project's spaces on GitHub, and when someone is representing the project in public.
